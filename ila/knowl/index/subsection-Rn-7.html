Example 1.1.7 3-Space.

When \(n=3\text{,}\) we can think of \(\R^3\) as the space we (appear to) live in. We can do so because every point in space can be represented by an ordered triple of real numbers, namely, its \(x\)-, \(y\)-, and \(z\)-coordinates.
