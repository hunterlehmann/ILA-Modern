Instructions.
Drag the blue points in the left-hand (2D) view. The red curve is the zero set of the best-fit function \(f(x,y) = x^2 + Ay^2 + Bxy + Cx + Dy + E\text{,}\) and the text shows its coefficients and the quantity minimized, \(\sum_i f(x_i,y_i)^2\text{.}\)
🔗
The right-hand (3D) view shows the graph \(z = f(x,y)\) in red; the violet segments are the errors \(f(x_i,y_i)\) at the data points. Drag the background of the 3D view to rotate it.
